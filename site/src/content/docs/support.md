Timelapse Switch is free and open source, and it will stay that way: every feature is in the free download, and the plugin itself never asks for money. If it saves you editing time, you can help it grow.

## Tip

- **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/miguetronic)**: a one-off tip or a monthly one, no account needed.

Tips pay for the time that goes into fixes, new features, and testing on more systems and graphics cards.

## Fund a piece of work

Some work has a clear cost and a clear result, and can be funded on its own:

- **Testing and polishing Windows or macOS**, today published as beta.
- **A feature you need**, from the ideas on GitHub or your own.
- **Priority support** for a studio or a team that depends on the plugin.

Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/miguetronic/timelapse-switch/discussions), or with a message on Ko-fi. Funded features land in the free, open source plugin, for everyone.

## Help without money

- **Star** the repository on GitHub, and tell others who record long sessions.
- **Report how it works** on your system, especially on [Windows and macOS](https://github.com/miguetronic/timelapse-switch/issues/new?template=beta-platform.yml).
- **Report bugs** with your OBS log, and suggest ideas in [Discussions](https://github.com/miguetronic/timelapse-switch/discussions).
- **Contribute** code or documentation: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/miguetronic/timelapse-switch/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
