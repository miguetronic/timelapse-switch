## How it works

Think of a tape recorder with a fast-forward pedal: while you hold the pedal, the tape records only 1 of every 4 moments, so that part plays 4 times faster. OBS encoders count frames instead of looking at the clock, so a section with fewer frames simply plays faster. In timelapse the sound is silent, so sped-up audio never squeaks.
