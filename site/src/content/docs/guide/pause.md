---
title: Pause and resume
description: Pause a recording in real time or in timelapse, and choose how to resume.
---

Press **Pause** in the dock (or the "Pause or resume" hotkey). Nothing is recorded while paused: the pause takes no time in the final video.

While paused, the speed buttons choose **how to resume**: the dock shows "Resume in: Real time ×2 ×4 ×8 ×16", and the main button names the choice, for example **Resume in timelapse ×4**.

- Pause as often as you like: quick pauses one after another are fine.
- Stopping while paused finishes the file normally.
- Resuming at another speed than before adds a chapter for that speed.
