## The dock

Open **Docks > Timelapse Switch**. The dock records **its own file**, with the encoder and settings of your OBS profile; OBS's own **Start Recording** button keeps working as always, at normal speed.

## Record

1. Press **Record with Timelapse Switch**. The dock shows **REAL TIME** and starts counting.
2. When the slow part begins, press **Switch to timelapse**. Choose the speed under it: **×2**, **×4**, **×8** or **×16**.
3. When something worth watching happens again, press **Back to real time**.
4. Press **Stop and save**. The file is finished: no editing needed.

The dock shows two clocks: **Recorded** (the time you spent) and **Final video** (how long the file will be). The difference is the waiting you skipped.

## The file

Recordings go to your OBS profile's recording folder (**Settings > Output > Recording path**), with " tswitch" at the end of the name. In Hybrid MP4 (**Settings > Output > Recording Format**) each change of speed adds a **chapter**, so video players and editors can jump straight to each part.

In timelapse the sound is silent: sped-up sound is just noise, and silence keeps the audio track in step with the picture.
